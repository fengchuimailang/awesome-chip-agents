## English

### Papers

[TACS-RF: Tool-driven Agentic Circuit Synthesis of RF and mmWave Modules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31882) (2026-10-22, preprint) — The framework uses an LLM coding agent with Virtuoso/Spectre, Calibre, Cadence EMX, and Ansys HFSS. The paper reports a semi-autonomous 240–251 GHz transmitter design in a 90 nm SiGe BiCMOS process and was accepted for presentation at the 2027 IEEE Radio and Wireless Week SiRF symposium.

## 简体中文

### 论文

[TACS-RF: Tool-driven Agentic Circuit Synthesis of RF and mmWave Modules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31882)（2026-10-22，预印本）— 该框架使用 LLM 编码 Agent 调用 Virtuoso/Spectre、Calibre、Cadence EMX 和 Ansys HFSS。论文报告了在 90 nm SiGe BiCMOS 工艺下半自主设计 240–251 GHz 发射机，并获 2027 IEEE Radio and Wireless Week SiRF 研讨会接收作报告。
