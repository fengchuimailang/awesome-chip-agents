## English

### Papers

[AgenticSizing: A Large Language Model-based Multi-Agent Framework for Analog Circuit Sizing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25873) (2026-09-22, preprint) — Analyzes circuit topology and functional blocks, reuses design knowledge, and coordinates specialized sizing agents through simulation feedback; evaluated on eight circuits, with a [public implementation](https://github.com/aprilaihub/agentic-analog-sizing).

### Open-source Projects

[AgenticSizing](https://github.com/aprilaihub/agentic-analog-sizing) — Public multi-agent analog circuit sizing implementation with topology analysis, reusable design knowledge, specialized sizing agents, and simulation-driven optimization.

## 简体中文

### 论文

[AgenticSizing: A Large Language Model-based Multi-Agent Framework for Analog Circuit Sizing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25873)（2026-09-22，预印本）— 分析电路拓扑与功能块、复用设计知识，并通过仿真反馈协调专门的尺寸优化 Agent；在八个电路上进行评测，提供[公开实现](https://github.com/aprilaihub/agentic-analog-sizing)。

### 开源项目

[AgenticSizing](https://github.com/aprilaihub/agentic-analog-sizing) — 公开的多 Agent 模拟电路尺寸优化实现，包含拓扑分析、设计知识复用、专门的尺寸优化 Agent 和仿真驱动优化。
