## English

### Benchmarks and Datasets

[Long-Horizon Analog Design Bench: Benchmarking Agents on Hours-Long Analog and Mixed-Signal Circuit Design Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33356) (2026-09-27, preprint) — Evaluates agents on 50 transistor-level design tasks contributed by 17 chip designers, using an open-source simulator and an isolated verifier to check electrical specifications over hours-long runs.

## 简体中文

### 基准与数据集

[Long-Horizon Analog Design Bench: Benchmarking Agents on Hours-Long Analog and Mixed-Signal Circuit Design Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33356)（2026-09-27，预印本）— 使用 17 位芯片设计者贡献的 50 项晶体管级设计任务评测 Agent，通过开源仿真器和隔离的验证器，在数小时运行过程中检查电气规格是否满足。
