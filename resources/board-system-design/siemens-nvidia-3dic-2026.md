### News and Updates

**2026-10-21 — Siemens and NVIDIA:** [Siemens expands agentic AI collaboration to tackle 3D IC power and thermal challenges with NVIDIA](https://news.siemens.com/en-us/siemens-nvidia-gtc-3d-ic-thermal-ai/) — Announced a Fuse EDA AI Agent workflow coordinating nine stages across seven EDA tools for 3D IC electrothermal codesign, diagnosing missed targets and iterating design alternatives with physics-based solvers; capabilities and productivity figures are vendor-reported.
