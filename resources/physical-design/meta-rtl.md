## English

The paper presents an EDA-grounded method for evolving an RTL optimization agent's search harness while keeping the underlying model, EDA flow, and per-design budget fixed. Against Dr. RTL, the authors report reduced aggregate WNS/TNS violations on 20 development designs and further gains on 10 unseen IC-RTL designs, with lower synthesized area. This is a recent preprint; the reported comparisons are author-evaluated.

## 简体中文

论文提出一种基于 EDA 反馈的方法，在固定底层模型、EDA 流程和单设计预算的条件下，迭代改进 RTL 优化 Agent 的搜索流程。作者报告称，与 Dr. RTL 相比，该方法在 20 个开发设计上降低了总体 WNS/TNS 违例，并在 10 个未见 IC-RTL 设计上进一步改善，同时降低综合面积。该文为近期预印本，比较结果由论文作者评测。
