## English

### Papers

[LATCH: LLM-Assisted Timing Closure Harness for Post-Route FPGA Optimisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16625) (2026-10-12, preprint) — Separates LLM orchestration from deterministic FPGA implementation and evaluation, then compares single-step control strategies and multi-iteration search across sixteen designs. The authors provide the [implementation](https://github.com/aprilaihub/LATCH).

## 简体中文

### 论文

[LATCH: LLM-Assisted Timing Closure Harness for Post-Route FPGA Optimisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16625)（2026-10-12，预印本）— 将 LLM 编排与确定性的 FPGA 实现和评估分开，并在 16 个设计上比较单步控制策略和多轮搜索。作者公开了[实现代码](https://github.com/aprilaihub/LATCH)。
