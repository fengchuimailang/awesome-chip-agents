## English

### Benchmarks and Datasets

[Introducing Build-A-Chip-Bench: from workload specification to GDS](https://chipagents.ai/research/build-a-chip-bench) (2026-10) — ChipAgents benchmark with contributions from NVIDIA, evaluating agents from workload specification through physical implementation; functional correctness, routability, hold timing, and DRC gate PPA scoring. Results are developer-reported; as of 2026-10-24, the live leaderboard is listed as coming soon.

## 简体中文

### 基准与数据集

[Introducing Build-A-Chip-Bench: from workload specification to GDS](https://chipagents.ai/research/build-a-chip-bench)（2026-10）— ChipAgents 开发、NVIDIA 参与贡献的基准，评测 Agent 从工作负载规格到物理实现的能力；设计须通过功能正确性、可布线性、保持时间和 DRC 检查，再按 PPA 评分。结果由开发方报告；截至 2026-10-24，实时排行榜仍标为即将推出。
