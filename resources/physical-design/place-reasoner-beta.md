## English

### Open-source Projects

[PlaceReasoner-Beta and PlaceReasoner-Bench](https://github.com/fenggev56/PlaceReasoner-Beta) — An agent framework and open benchmark for macro placement using floorplan context, geometric checks, and physical-design feedback.

### Benchmarks and Datasets

[PlaceReasoner-Beta: Reasoning-Driven Macro Placement and Benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21263) (2026) — PlaceReasoner-Bench contains 16 tasks across eight open designs and evaluates routed PPA and design-rule violations.

## 简体中文

### 开源项目

[PlaceReasoner-Beta 与 PlaceReasoner-Bench](https://github.com/fenggev56/PlaceReasoner-Beta) — 面向宏单元布局的 Agent 框架和开放基准，结合 floorplan 上下文、几何检查与物理设计反馈。

[PlaceReasoner-Beta: Reasoning-Driven Macro Placement and Benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21263)（2026）— PlaceReasoner-Bench 涵盖八个开放设计的 16 项任务，并使用布线后 PPA 和设计规则违规进行评测。
