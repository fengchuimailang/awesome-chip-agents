## English

### Papers

[A Closer Look at Agentic BBO: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Black-Box Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12183) (2026-10-08, preprint) — AgenticBBO-Bench evaluates black-box optimization agents under a unified finite-budget protocol across synthetic functions, hyperparameter optimization, database tuning, chip design, and molecular design. The [repository](https://github.com/lamda-bbo/agentic-bbo) includes the benchmark code. This is a cross-domain benchmark, not a chip-design-only suite.

## 简体中文

### 论文

[A Closer Look at Agentic BBO: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Black-Box Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12183)（2026-10-08，预印本）— AgenticBBO-Bench 使用统一的有限预算协议评测黑盒优化 Agent，覆盖合成函数、超参数优化、数据库调优、芯片设计和分子设计。[仓库](https://github.com/lamda-bbo/agentic-bbo)提供基准代码。该基准覆盖多个领域，并非专为芯片设计建立。
