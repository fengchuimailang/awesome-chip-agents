## English

### Papers

[Agentic Architect: An Agentic AI Framework for Architecture Design Exploration and Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25083) (first public 2026-04-28; revised 2026-10-12, preprint) — Starts from seed designs and uses LLM-driven code evolution, cycle-accurate simulation, and hardware cost models to explore prefetching, cache replacement, and branch prediction. Evaluation feedback guides candidate selection under area and power budgets. Results come from simulation and cost modeling; seed selection, training workloads, and hardware constraints affect the search outcomes. The catalog groups this work under its April debut, retaining the October revision here.

## 简体中文

### 论文

[Agentic Architect: An Agentic AI Framework for Architecture Design Exploration and Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25083)（首次公开于 2026-04-28，2026-10-12 修订，预印本）— 从种子设计出发，结合 LLM 驱动的代码进化、周期级精确仿真与硬件成本模型，探索预取器、缓存替换和分支预测设计。评估反馈指导面积与功耗预算下的候选选择。结果来自仿真和成本建模；种子选择、训练工作负载与硬件约束都会影响搜索结果。目录按首次公开日期归入 4 月，并在此保留 10 月修订信息。
