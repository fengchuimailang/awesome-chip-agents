## English

### Papers

[Agentic Design Space Exploration for Joint Hardware Configuration Selection and Mapping of AI Inference Workloads on Heterogeneous Edge SoCs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07191) (published at MLCAD 2026) — TraceDSE uses an LLM proposer and a trace-analysis critic to jointly map inference workloads and configure processing units on heterogeneous SoCs. The paper evaluates four workloads on Intel Meteor Lake and reports improvements over two black-box optimization baselines.

## 简体中文

### 论文

[Agentic Design Space Exploration for Joint Hardware Configuration Selection and Mapping of AI Inference Workloads on Heterogeneous Edge SoCs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07191)（发表于 MLCAD 2026）— TraceDSE 使用 LLM 提案 Agent 和分析执行轨迹的评审 Agent，在异构 SoC 上联合映射推理负载并配置处理单元。论文在 Intel Meteor Lake 上评测四种负载，并报告了相较两种黑盒优化基线的改进。
