### Benchmarks and Datasets

[HWE-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents on Real-World Hardware Bug Repair Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14709) (2026) — Repository-level benchmark with 417 fail-to-pass bug-fix tasks from six open-source hardware projects; [code and dataset download instructions](https://github.com/pku-liang/hwe-bench) cover Verilog, SystemVerilog, and Chisel.
