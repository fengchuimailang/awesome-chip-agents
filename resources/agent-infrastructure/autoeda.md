## English

AutoEDA proposes an MCP-based microservice architecture for natural-language EDA-flow automation. It separates task decomposition, tool selection, execution, and error handling across LLM agents and studies a locally fine-tuned model for RTL-to-GDSII workflows. Accuracy and token-efficiency comparisons are reported by the paper's authors. This record catalogs the paper; an accessible public implementation repository was not verified during this review.

The date is the first arXiv submission, August 1, 2025. The paper was revised on February 24, 2026.

## 简体中文

AutoEDA 提出基于 MCP 的微服务架构，用于自然语言 EDA 流程自动化。它通过 LLM Agent 分解任务、选择工具、执行并处理错误，同时研究面向 RTL-to-GDSII 流程的本地微调模型。准确率和 token 效率对比属于论文作者报告。本条目收录论文，本次核实时未确认可访问的公开实现仓库。

日期采用首次提交 arXiv 的 2025 年 8 月 1 日；论文于 2026 年 2 月 24 日修订。
