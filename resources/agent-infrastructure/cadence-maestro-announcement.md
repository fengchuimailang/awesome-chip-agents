## English

### News and Updates

**2026-10-26 — Cadence:** [Cadence Introduces Maestro, a Domain-Specialized AI Model Portfolio to Revolutionize Agentic Chip and System Design](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2026/cadence-introduces-maestro-a-domain-specialized-ai-model.html) — Announced post-trained LLMs, decision models, and self-improving agent skills connected to Cadence AI Super Agents and EDA/SDA tools through reinforcement-learning feedback. The environment combines specialized, frontier, and open-weight models and supports cloud or customer-controlled on-premises deployment. Cadence reports initial workflow gains of up to 30% in quality of results and up to 5× token efficiency against general-purpose foundation models; these are vendor-reported results. The release limits initial access to selected customers in Q4 2026.

## 简体中文

### 新闻与动态

**2026-10-26 — Cadence：** [Cadence Introduces Maestro, a Domain-Specialized AI Model Portfolio to Revolutionize Agentic Chip and System Design](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2026/cadence-introduces-maestro-a-domain-specialized-ai-model.html) — 宣布通过强化学习反馈，将后训练 LLM、决策模型和可自我改进的 Agent 技能与 Cadence AI Super Agents、EDA / SDA 工具相连接。该环境结合专用、前沿及开放权重模型，支持云端或客户控制的本地部署。Cadence 报告初步工作流结果相对通用基础模型最高提升 30% 的设计结果质量、最高达到 5 倍 token 效率；这些数字属于厂商报告。公告中的首批模型仅向 2026 年第四季度的部分早期访问客户提供。
