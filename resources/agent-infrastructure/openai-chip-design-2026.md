## English

### News and Updates

**2026-11-18 — OpenAI:** [Advancing chip design with OpenAI models](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/) reports model-assisted RTL changes to OpenAI's Jalapeño accelerator and AMD designs, including up to 14% PPA improvement on AMD flagship GPU IP, as reported by OpenAI. It also describes evaluations on PicoRV32 and Ibex for RTL optimization and specification-to-design tasks. The public evaluation uses simulation, hidden workloads, and Yosys timing estimates; OpenAI notes that specification-based designs may omit features and that physical-synthesis timing checks would make the evaluation more representative of production design.

## 简体中文

### 新闻与动态

**2026-11-18 — OpenAI：** [Advancing chip design with OpenAI models](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/) 报告了模型辅助修改 OpenAI 的 Jalapeño 加速器及 AMD 芯片设计中的 RTL，并称 AMD 旗舰 GPU IP 的 PPA 最高提升 14%。文章还介绍了在 PicoRV32 和 Ibex 上进行 RTL 优化与从规格设计芯片的评测。公开评测使用仿真、隐藏工作负载和 Yosys 时序估算；OpenAI 指出，从规格生成的设计可能省略原有功能，而加入物理综合后的时序检查会更贴近生产设计。
