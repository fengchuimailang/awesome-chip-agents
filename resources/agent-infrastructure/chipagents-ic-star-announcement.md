## English

### News and Updates

**2026-10-27 — ChipAgents:** [ChipAgents Expands Full-Stack Agentic AI Across Digital and Analog Chip Design](https://chipagents.ai/newsroom/chipagents-ic-star-full-stack-agentic-ai) — Announced expansion of its platform around IC-star, combining persistent design knowledge, agent and tool orchestration, models informed by physical design behavior, and dynamic model routing. Described workflows span RTL design, verification, debugging, and PPA optimization, as well as analog schematics, simulation, layout, and physical verification. The announcement also describes collaboration with NVIDIA to fine-tune Nemotron models. Platform capabilities, deployment counts, and customer productivity figures are vendor-reported, rather than independent evaluation results.

## 简体中文

### 新闻与动态

**2026-10-27 — ChipAgents：** [ChipAgents Expands Full-Stack Agentic AI Across Digital and Analog Chip Design](https://chipagents.ai/newsroom/chipagents-ic-star-full-stack-agentic-ai) — 宣布围绕 IC-star 扩展平台，结合持久设计知识、Agent 与工具编排、考虑物理设计行为的模型以及动态模型路由。公告描述的工作流涵盖 RTL 设计、验证、调试、PPA 优化，以及模拟电路原理图、仿真、版图与物理验证，并介绍了与 NVIDIA 微调 Nemotron 模型的合作。平台能力、部署数量和客户效率数字均为厂商报告，并非独立评测结果。
