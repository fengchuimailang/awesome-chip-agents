## English

### Papers

[ChipOmni: A General-Purpose AI Agent for Electronic Design Automation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13020) (2026-11-09, preprint) — Combines multimodal understanding, dynamic planning, multi-agent collaboration, and verification-driven tool interaction. The paper evaluates analog circuit, RTL, testbench, and formal-assertion generation, and reports a long-horizon natural-language-to-micro-SoC-GDSII case study.

## 简体中文

### 论文

[ChipOmni: A General-Purpose AI Agent for Electronic Design Automation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13020)（2026-11-09，预印本）— 结合多模态理解、动态规划、多 Agent 协作和验证反馈工具交互。论文评测了模拟电路、RTL、测试平台及形式断言生成，并报告了从自然语言规格推进到微型 SoC GDSII 的长程案例。
