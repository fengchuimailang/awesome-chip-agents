## English

### Benchmarks and Datasets

[SynthAgentic-Eval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KSU-HW-SEC/SynthAgentic-Eval) (GLSVLSI 2026) — Public synthesis-aware evaluation data for 32 language models on 202 Verilog tasks over five attempts, including per-attempt results and golden synthesis references; see the accompanying [analysis pipeline](https://github.com/owenfucell/SynthAgentic).

## 简体中文

### 基准与数据集

[SynthAgentic-Eval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KSU-HW-SEC/SynthAgentic-Eval)（GLSVLSI 2026）— 面向综合感知评测的公开数据，记录 32 个语言模型在 202 个 Verilog 任务上各尝试五次的结果，并提供每次尝试的结果和黄金综合参考；配套[分析流程](https://github.com/owenfucell/SynthAgentic)。
