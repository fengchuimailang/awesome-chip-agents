## English

### Benchmarks and Datasets

[Comprehensive Verilog Design Problems (CVDP)](https://github.com/NVlabs/cvdp_benchmark) — NVIDIA benchmark for RTL coding and verification tasks, including generation, editing, and debugging. [Si2's coalition announcement](https://si2.org/si2-llm-benchmarking-coalition-kicks-off/) (2025-06-26) describes extending CVDP problems and metrics. The [LBC-bench leaderboards](https://lbc-bench.si2.org/) provide code-generation, code-comprehension, and heavy-context views, with links to testbench and dataset versions. The coalition announcement date is not used as the benchmark's initial release date.

## 简体中文

### 基准与数据集

[Comprehensive Verilog Design Problems (CVDP)](https://github.com/NVlabs/cvdp_benchmark) — NVIDIA 发布的 RTL 编码与验证基准，覆盖生成、编辑和调试等任务。[Si2 联盟公告](https://si2.org/si2-llm-benchmarking-coalition-kicks-off/)（2025-06-26）介绍了扩展 CVDP 题目与评测指标的计划。[LBC-bench 排行榜](https://lbc-bench.si2.org/)提供代码生成、代码理解及重上下文分类，并链接测试平台与数据集版本。联盟公告日期不作为基准首次发布日期。
