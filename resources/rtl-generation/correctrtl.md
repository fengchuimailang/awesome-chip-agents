## English

### Papers

[CorrectRTL: Boost RTL Generation Quality Through Automated Self-Correction with Instruction Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24977) (2026-10-18; ICCAD 2026) — Separates structural planning from block implementation and combines snippet retrieval with SVA-guided self-correction; introduces the 200-design ScaleBench, with [public benchmark data and evaluation tools](https://github.com/hkust-zhiyao/CorrectRTL).

## 简体中文

### 论文

[CorrectRTL: Boost RTL Generation Quality Through Automated Self-Correction with Instruction Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24977)（2026-10-18；ICCAD 2026）— 将结构规划与模块内逻辑实现分离，结合代码片段检索与 SVA 引导的自我修正；提出包含 200 个设计的 ScaleBench，提供[公开基准数据和评测工具](https://github.com/hkust-zhiyao/CorrectRTL)。
